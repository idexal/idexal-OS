---

Give ChatGPT your organization's idexal Os skills, plugins, and connections.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open MCP settings">
In ChatGPT, open `Settings > MCP servers` ([open settings](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Connectors)).
</Step>
<Step title="Paste the URL">

```text
https://api.idexal.com/mcp/agent
```

</Step>
<Step title="Sign in">
Start OAuth from the connection prompt, sign in to idexal Os, and pick your organization.
</Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Switch organizations">
    In `Settings > MCP servers`, disconnect `idexal Os`, then connect again and pick another organization.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Support status">
    Setup only: these steps follow idexal Os's OAuth profile, but native end-to-end proof is not complete. See the [MCP Gateway reference](/cloud/run-in-the-cloud/cloud-mcp#reference).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
