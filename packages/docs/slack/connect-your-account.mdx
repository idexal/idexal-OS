**Private alpha.** Your administrator must [set up the Slack app](/slack/set-up-the-slack-app) before this works.
</Note>

## Connect once

1. Open **Your Connections** in idexal Os and connect your own Slack account.
2. If you connected before the assistant was installed, disconnect **your personal Slack connection**, then connect it again once. This temporarily interrupts that connection; it does not require signing everyone out or reinstalling the bot.
3. Authorize using the Slack workspace and account you will use to message `@idexalos`.

This links your Slack identity to your idexal Os membership. Before linking, the assistant asks you to connect. `/idexalos` returns a connection link; following the link alone does not complete authorization.

## Verify it works

1. Open `idexalos` under Slack's agents/apps and send a DM: “Reply with a short hello to confirm my connection works.”
2. Wait for the finished answer in the thread. “On it” only confirms that a request started.
3. In an allowed test channel, run `/invite @idexalos`, then mention it with the same test request. Confirm it appears in mention autocomplete and replies in the selected reply mode.
4. Administrators: check the connector's **Last 24 hours** counts for completed, active, need-connection, and failed requests.

Once those checks pass, try a task such as “Summarize this thread” or “Draft a reply.” Connected-service access depends on your own permissions. If the assistant needs a permission decision, continue in idexal Os Web; Slack does not approve it for you.

Something off? See [Troubleshooting](/slack/troubleshooting).
