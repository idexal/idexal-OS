An [MCP connection](/integrations/mcp) gives your client skills and tools. A client plugin also adds your organization's AI Gateway models to the model picker, with one sign-in.

## OpenCode — ready

Install [`opencode-idexal-os`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/opencode-idexal-os) in OpenCode 2. No desktop app needed.

```sh
opencode plugin add opencode-idexal-os
opencode auth login idexalos
```

Choose **Browser**, sign in, pick your organization, and approve the matching code. OpenCode then loads your models, skills, and tools every time it starts.

Requirements, checks, self-hosted setup, and sign-out: [OpenCode plugin guide](/model-context-protocol/opencode-plugin).
